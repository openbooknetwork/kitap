# Traditional Chinese (Taiwan)

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write the vocabulary of Taiwan's schoolbooks, and the forms of foreign names they use: 拿破崙, 雪梨, 凱末爾,
  伊斯坦堡. A name they lack follows the National Academy for Educational Research's term database. A title follows
  the name with no mark: 恩維爾帕夏.
- Name a disputed event by the term that names both sides: 國共內戰 for the Chinese Civil War, 韓戰 for the Korean
  War.
- A Japanese or Korean name takes its characters in traditional form: 伊藤博文, 東京, 李舜臣, 首爾.
- Punctuation is full width: ，。、：；？！（）. Quotes are 「」, with 『』 inside them. The title of a book, a treaty
  or a law takes 《》: 《洛桑條約》.
- Dates read year, month, day with 年月日, and digits touch the characters around them: 1921年8月26日, 第57團. Years
  are Common Era years, and a year before it takes 西元前: 西元前221年.
- A count under one hundred in running text takes Chinese numerals: 七歲, 十天. Years, dates, measures and labels
  take the digits 0–9. A round number above ten thousand takes 萬: 25萬. Others group thousands with a
  comma: 315,500. A range takes 至 and keeps one form at both ends: 1919至1923年, 250,000至315,500.
- The parts of a foreign name are joined with ‧ (U+2027): 穆斯塔法‧凱末爾. Times of day read 上午8時30分.
- A count takes the measure word of its noun: 三艘軍艦, 四十門大砲, 兩個營.
- Units take their Taiwan names: 公尺, 公分, 公釐, 公里, 公斤.
- Anatomy terms take the form of Taiwan's science schoolbooks, and the term database's form where they have none:
  左心室, 胰臟, 脛骨.
- Write the active voice where English uses the passive, and keep 被 for something the subject suffers:
  學校建於1900年, 伊茲密爾被希臘軍隊占領. Leave out a pronoun the sentence reads clearly without, and drop 們 where a
  number or 許多 already shows the plural: 許多城市. An animal is 牠.
