# Traditional Chinese (Taiwan)

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write the vocabulary of Taiwan's schoolbooks, and the forms of foreign names they use: 拿破崙, 雪梨, 凱末爾,
  伊斯坦堡. A name they lack follows the National Academy for Educational Research's term database.
- Name a disputed event by the term that names both sides: 國共內戰 for the Chinese Civil War, 韓戰 for the Korean
  War.
- Punctuation is full width: ，。、：；？！（）. Quotes are 「」, with 『』 inside them.
- Dates read year, month, day with 年月日: 1921年8月26日. Years are Common Era years.
- A count under one hundred in running text takes Chinese numerals: 七歲, 十天. Years, dates, measures and labels
  take the digits 0–9. A round number above ten thousand takes 萬: 25萬. Others group thousands with a
  comma: 315,500. A range keeps one form at both ends: 250,000至315,500.
- The parts of a foreign name are joined with ‧ (U+2027): 穆斯塔法‧凱末爾. Times of day read 上午8時30分.
