# Portuguese

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write Brazilian Portuguese in the spelling of the 1990 Orthographic Agreement, with the ABL's VOLP as the
  reference. Words and accents are Brazilian: fato, registro, trem, fenômeno, Salônica, esôfago, abdômen, fêmur.
  An action in progress takes the gerund: os soldados estavam cavando trincheiras.
- The text and the interface address the reader as você, with the verb in the third person: Escolha uma batalha,
  Clique no mapa. A button names its action in the infinitive: Começar, Ver o mapa.
- The possessive seu belongs to the reader. Another person's possession takes dele or dela: o exército dele.
  A part of the body takes the article in place of the English possessive: Quando você corre, o coração bate mais
  rápido.
- An unstressed pronoun goes before the verb, as Brazilian writing places it: a cidade se rendeu, os aliados o
  cercaram. At the start of a sentence it follows the verb: Renderam-se no dia seguinte.
- A name takes the form of Brazilian schoolbooks and encyclopedias: Istambul, Galípoli, Dardanelos, Tessalônica
  (então Salônica). An epithet follows the name after a comma: Pedro, o Grande. Pasha is Paxá after the name:
  Enver Paxá.
- A link covers the name. A contraction or a crase stays outside it: na [Grécia](@greece), do [Cairo](@cairo).
  A name that takes an article takes à after a verb of motion, and a name without one takes a: à [Grécia](@greece),
  a [Atenas](@athens). The return shows which: volto da Grécia, volto de Atenas.
- Months, days, nationalities and offices take a small letter: 26 de agosto, os turcos, o sultão Mehmed II.
  A title or a label capitalizes its first word and its names only: A campanha de Galípoli. The names of wars,
  treaties and empires capitalize each main word: Primeira Guerra Mundial, Tratado de Versalhes, Império Otomano.
- Quotes are “ ”, with ‘ ’ inside them: “O coronel escreveu: ‘Resistam’.”
- Numbers group thousands with a dot and take a decimal comma: 250.000, 1,5 km. Years stay together: 1921.
  A percent sign follows the number directly: 30%.
- Dates read day, month, year, joined by de: 26 de agosto de 1921. The first day of a month takes the ordinal
  sign º (U+00BA): 1º de maio de 1915. A range takes an en dash: 1919–1923. Times of day read 8h30, 16h.
- Centuries take Roman numerals: século XIX. The 1920s are a década de 1920 or os anos 1920.
