# German

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write the standard German of Germany under the rules of the Rat für deutsche Rechtschreibung: Straße, Januar.
  A noun for a group of people takes the masculine plural or both forms: die Soldaten, Bürgerinnen und Bürger.
- The text and the interface address the reader as Sie: Blättern Sie mit den Pfeiltasten.
- A name takes the form German schoolbooks and the Duden use: Istanbul, Izmir, Dardanellen, Moskau, Peter der
  Große. A name in Latin letters keeps its letters and marks: Çanakkale, Eskişehir, İnönü. A Cyrillic name follows
  the Duden transcription: Chruschtschow, Jelzin.
- A title follows the name as its own word: İsmet Pascha. A ruler's number is an ordinal with a dot: Mehmed II.,
  unter Wilhelm II.
- A link covers the name as the sentence writes it, genitive s included, and the article stays outside it:
  die Straßen [Ankaras](@ankara), in der [Türkei](@turkey). A name that ends in s, ß, x or z marks the genitive
  with an apostrophe alone: Aristoteles’ Schüler.
- An adjective in the name of an event or a state takes a capital: der Erste Weltkrieg, der Dreißigjährige Krieg,
  das Osmanische Reich. Other adjectives of peoples and places stay in lower case: die osmanische Flotte.
- Quotes are „…“, with ‚…‘ inside them. A dash in a sentence is an en dash with a space on each side: –.
- Numbers take a decimal comma and group thousands with a no break space: 1,5 km, 1 500, 250 000. A year stays
  whole: 1921. A per cent sign and a unit follow a no break space: 1 %, 3 cm.
- Dates read day with a dot, month, year: am 26. August 1921. A range reads 1919–1923 or von 1919 bis 1923.
  Centuries are ordinals: im 19. Jahrhundert. A year before Christ takes v. Chr.: 490 v. Chr.
- Times of day take Uhr: 8 Uhr, 8:30 Uhr.
- Anatomy takes the German word of biology schoolbooks where one is in common use: Herzkammer over Ventrikel,
  Großhirnrinde over Kortex, Speiseröhre, Schilddrüse, Nebennieren.
- Wars take the names German histories give them: der Türkische Befreiungskrieg, die Perserkriege, der Große
  Türkenkrieg. Ottoman is osmanisch. The Allies of the First World War are die Entente.
- An English billion is eine Milliarde.
