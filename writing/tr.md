# Turkish

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- A suffix on a proper name or a number follows a typographic apostrophe: Ankara’ya, 26 Ağustos’ta, 1920’de.
- Numbers group thousands with a dot and take a decimal comma: 250.000, 1,5 km.
- Dates read day, month, year: 26 Ağustos 1921. A range takes an en dash: 1919–1923.
- Words stay Turkish where Turkish has the word in common use: karıncık over ventrikül, taarruz over ofansif.
