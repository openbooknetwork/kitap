# Simplified Chinese

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write the vocabulary of mainland China's schoolbooks, and the forms of foreign names that Xinhua's name
  dictionaries give (世界人名翻译大辞典, 外国地名译名手册): 奥斯曼帝国, 伊斯坦布尔, 悉尼, 凯末尔. A title follows the
  name with no mark: 恩维尔帕夏.
- Name a disputed event by the term that names both sides: 国共内战 for the Chinese Civil War, 朝鲜战争 for the Korean
  War, 上海战役 for the taking of Shanghai in 1949. A state keeps the name the source gives it: 中华人民共和国, and
  中华民国 after 1949 too.
- A Japanese or Korean name takes its characters in simplified form: 伊藤博文, 东京, 李舜臣, 首尔.
- Punctuation is full width: ，。、：；？！（）. Quotes are “ ”, with ‘ ’ inside them. The title of a book, a treaty or
  a law takes 《》: 《洛桑条约》.
- The parts of a foreign name are joined with · (U+00B7): 穆斯塔法·凯末尔.
- Dates read year, month, day with 年月日, and digits touch the characters around them: 1921年8月26日, 第57团. A year
  before the Common Era takes 公元前: 公元前221年. A decade names its century: 20世纪20年代. Times of day read
  上午8时30分.
- A count under one hundred in running text takes Chinese numerals: 七岁, 十天. Years, dates, measures and labels
  take the digits 0–9. A round number above ten thousand takes 万 or 亿: 25万, 3亿. Others of five digits or more
  group thousands with a comma: 315,500. Four digits stay whole: 1500.
- A range of years or dates takes — (U+2014): 1919—1923年. A range of numbers takes ～, with 万 or the unit at both
  ends: 20万～30万.
- A count takes the measure word of its noun: 三艘军舰, 四十门大炮, 两个营.
- Units take their mainland names: 米, 厘米, 毫米, 公里, 千克.
- Anatomy terms take the form of mainland biology schoolbooks, and the 人体解剖学名词 that CNTERM approves where they
  have none: 左心室, 胰腺, 胫骨.
- Write the active voice where English uses the passive, and keep 被 for something the subject suffers:
  学校建于1900年, 伊兹密尔被希腊军队占领. Leave out a pronoun the sentence reads clearly without, and drop 们 where a
  number or 许多 already shows the plural: 许多城市. The interface addresses the reader as 你.
