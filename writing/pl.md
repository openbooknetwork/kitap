# Polish

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- The text and the interface address the reader in the second person singular and leave the pronoun out:
  Przewijaj strzałkami. A pronoun that stays takes a capital: Twój.
- A place outside Poland takes the name in the official list of the Komisja Standaryzacji Nazw Geograficznych:
  Stambuł, Saloniki, Dardanele, Ateny, Kijów. A name in Latin letters keeps its letters and marks: Çanakkale,
  İnönü. A Cyrillic name follows the Polish transcription: Chruszczow, Jelcyn.
- A ruler takes the Polish name and epithet, and the number without a dot: Mehmed II Zdobywca, Ludwik XIV,
  Wilhelm Zdobywca. A title follows the name: İsmet Pasza.
- A foreign name declines like a Polish noun with the same ending, and the link covers the declined word:
  w [Ankarze](@ankara), do [Stambułu](@istanbul), w [Salonikach](@salonica), wojska Kemala. An apostrophe stands
  before the ending only after a silent final letter: Joyce’a. A name ending in -i or -u stays as it is:
  na [Gallipoli](@gallipoli), w Baku.
- Wars and other historical events are written in lower case: pierwsza wojna światowa, wojna stuletnia. Each word
  of a state's name takes a capital: Imperium Osmańskie, Cesarstwo Rzymskie. Names of peoples and of the people of
  a town take a capital: Turcy, Grecy, Ateńczycy. Their adjectives and the months stay in lower case: turecki,
  sierpień.
- Quotes are „…”, with «…» inside them. A full stop goes after the closing quote.
- Numbers take a decimal comma, and from five digits group thousands with a no break space: 1,5 km, 1500,
  250 000.
- Dates read day, month in the genitive, year: 26 sierpnia 1921 r. A range reads w latach 1919–1923. Centuries
  take Roman numerals: w XIX wieku. A year before Christ takes p.n.e.: 490 p.n.e.
- Times of day read 8.30.
- An English passive whose actor is unknown or does not matter becomes the -no/-to form or an active verb:
  twierdzę zbudowano w 1453 r.
- Anatomy takes the Polish word of biology schoolbooks: wzgórze for the thalamus, podwzgórze, przysadka mózgowa,
  kora mózgowa, komora serca, nadnercza.
- Wars take the names Polish histories give them: wojny grecko-perskie, wielka wojna turecka, wojna krymska.
  Ottoman is osmański.
- An English billion is miliard.
