# Indonesian

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Spelling follows EYD, fifth edition (2022), and KBBI: Prancis, praktik, risiko, apotek.
- The interface addresses the reader as kamu, as the Ministry of Education's student books do. An instruction leaves
  the pronoun out: Pilih cerita.
- A name takes the form Indonesian schoolbooks use: Tiongkok, Belanda, Wina, Den Haag, Kekaisaran Romawi. A name from
  Islamic history takes its Indonesian form: Salahuddin Al-Ayyubi, Harun ar-Rasyid, Kesultanan Utsmaniyah.
- A nation, a people or a language starts with a capital, and the word before it stays lower case: bangsa Turki,
  suku Kurdi, bahasa Arab. A title before a name starts with a capital: Raja Louis XIV, Jenderal Allenby. On its own
  it is lower case: raja itu.
- Every word of a geographic name or a historical event starts with a capital: Selat Dardanela, Sungai Nil, Perang
  Dunia I, Revolusi Prancis. The generic word on its own is lower case: selat itu.
- A preposition stays outside the link: di [Ankara](@ankara), ke [Istanbul](@istanbul). A prefix before a capitalized
  word takes a hyphen: se-Eropa, anti-Inggris.
- A time takes pada and a place takes di: pada tahun 1921, di Ankara.
- A noun after a number, a quantifier or para keeps its single form: tiga kapal, banyak kota, para jenderal.
  Reduplication marks a plural only where nothing else does: kapal-kapal itu.
- Numbers group thousands with a dot and take a decimal comma: 250.000, 1,5 km. A year is four plain digits: 1921.
- Dates read day, month, year: 26 Agustus 1921. A range takes an en dash: 1919–1923. SM and M follow the year:
  218 SM, 724 M. Times of day read pukul 08.00.
- An ordinal takes ke- and a hyphen: abad ke-20, hari ke-3. A decade takes -an: tahun 1960-an. A ruler's number
  stays a Roman numeral after the name: Wilhelm II.
- Quotes are “ ”, with ‘ ’ inside them. An English dash inside a sentence becomes a comma or brackets.
- An organ or a structure takes the Indonesian word schoolbooks use: bilik jantung over ventrikel, serambi over
  atrium, pembuluh nadi over arteri, kerongkongan over esofagus.
