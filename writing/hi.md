# Hindi

These add to the rules for translating in [README.md](README.md).

- Write the everyday standard Hindi of schoolbooks. Prefer the word a child meets in them to a rarer Sanskrit or
  Persian synonym: सेना to वाहिनी or लश्कर. Address the reader as आप: देखें, पढ़ें.
- Spelling follows the Central Hindi Directorate's standard: an anusvara before a consonant of its class (हिंदी, संबंध,
  अंत), a chandrabindu where no vowel sign stands above the line (आँख, पाँच, हँसना) and an anusvara where one does
  (में, नहीं), verb forms with ए and ई (गए, गई, लिए), and the adjective with य (नया, नयी, नये).
- Arabic and Persian words that Hindi has absorbed take no nukta: कलम, किला, हजार. A foreign name keeps close to its
  own pronunciation, with a nukta for z and f: फ़्रांस, इज़मिर. ऑ marks the English open o: डॉक्टर, ऑस्ट्रेलिया.
- A case marker is a separate word after a noun and outside the link, and joins a pronoun: [अंकारा](@ankara) में,
  [इज़मिर](@izmir) का बंदरगाह, उसने.
- A name takes the form Hindi schoolbooks and encyclopaedias use: मुस्तफ़ा कमाल पाशा, अतातुर्क, इस्तांबुल, अंकारा.
  A ruler's number is an ordinal after the name: महमूद द्वितीय, बायज़ीद प्रथम.
- The Partition is भारत का विभाजन. A disputed area takes the neutral term of the source, translated word for word:
  पाकिस्तान प्रशासित कश्मीर, भारत प्रशासित कश्मीर.
- A sentence ends with the danda । (U+0964), with no space before it. Quotes are “ ”, with ‘ ’ inside them.
- Numbers use the digits 0–9 and group in lakhs and crores: 2,50,000. A decimal takes a point: 1.5 किलोमीटर. Units
  are written in Devanagari: किलोमीटर, मीटर.
- A round number takes हजार, लाख or करोड़: ढाई लाख. A million is 10 लाख and a billion 100 करोड़, so 1.5 million
  soldiers are 15 लाख सैनिक.
- Dates read day, month, year: 26 अगस्त 1921. Months are जनवरी, फ़रवरी, मार्च, अप्रैल, मई, जून, जुलाई, अगस्त, सितंबर,
  अक्तूबर, नवंबर, दिसंबर. A range in text reads 1919 से 1923 तक. A year before the Common Era takes ईसा पूर्व:
  326 ईसा पूर्व. Times of day read सुबह 10.30 बजे.
- Anatomy takes the terms of NCERT science books, with the everyday word in brackets the first time a page uses
  it: हृदय, फुफ्फुस (फेफड़ा), यकृत (जिगर), वृक्क (गुर्दा), आमाशय, अलिंद, निलय, कोशिका.
